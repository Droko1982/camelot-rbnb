Privacy and data processing policy

This policy applies to the personal data collected by Camelot (hereinafter “Camelot”), a lodging establishment located in Armenia, Quindío, Colombia, in compliance with Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1074 of 2015 and other personal data protection regulations.

Data controller

[information to be completed] · NIT/C.C. [information to be completed] · Armenia, Quindío, Colombia · Email: [information to be completed] · WhatsApp: [information to be completed].

What data we process

  • Identification and contact data: name, ID document or passport, nationality, phone number and email.
  • Booking data: dates, number of guests, room, payments and invoicing.
  • Data you voluntarily share with us, for example accessibility needs. If it is sensitive data, we only process it with your express authorization and in order to assist you.
  • Data of minors: only what is necessary for their registration, provided by their parents or legal representatives.

What we use it for

  • Managing your booking, your stay and our communication with you.
  • Complying with legal obligations: guest registration (Hotel Registration Card), reporting foreign guests to Migración Colombia (SIRE) and electronic invoicing with the DIAN.
  • The safety of our guests and the property.
  • Sending you news or offers, only if you authorize it; you may withdraw that authorization at any time.

Your rights

You may access, update and correct your data; request proof of your authorization; be informed of how your data is used; revoke your authorization or request its deletion when there is no legal duty to retain it; access it free of charge; and file complaints with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC).

How to exercise them

Write to us at [information to be completed] or via WhatsApp. We respond to inquiries within a maximum of 10 business days (extendable by 5 more) and to complaints within a maximum of 15 business days (extendable by 8 more), as provided in Law 1581 of 2012.

Retention and security

We keep your data for the duration of our relationship with you and for as long as tourism, accounting and tax regulations require. We apply reasonable security measures, and only the people who need it have access to it.

Visitors from the European Union, the United Kingdom and Brazil

If you reside there, we process your data on the basis of the performance of the lodging contract, compliance with legal obligations, our legitimate interest in security and, where applicable, your consent. You also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability (GDPR / UK GDPR / LGPD), and you may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Cookies and privacy on this site

  • No tracking or advertising cookies. This site does not use third-party analytics or social media pixels.
  • We store only your preferences in your own browser (local storage): language, light or dark mode, currency and whether you have already seen the privacy notice. You can delete them from your browser.
  • The OpenStreetMap map only loads if you click “Load the map.”
  • When you write to us via WhatsApp or through platforms such as Airbnb, those companies’ policies also apply.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States), which may log technical data such as your IP address for security reasons.

Lodging terms

  • Lodging contract. A booking confirmed in writing constitutes a lodging contract governed by Colombian law (the Commercial Code and tourism regulations). The rates shown on this site are indicative until we confirm them in writing.
  • Confirmation. Short and medium stays are confirmed with a 30% deposit. Long stays are confirmed with the first month and a refundable deposit, under the signed lodging agreement.
  • Registration. Every guest must present a valid ID (Colombian cédula or passport) upon arrival. Guest details are recorded on the Hotel Registration Card and, for foreign guests, reported to Migración Colombia.
  • Minors. Minors may stay only with their parents or with an adult authorized in writing, presenting a birth certificate (registro civil) or another document proving the relationship.
  • Occupancy. Each room has a maximum capacity. Visitors must be announced and registered; for security reasons, they may not stay overnight without registering as guests.
  • Payments and invoicing. Prices are settled in Colombian pesos and include any applicable taxes. We issue electronic invoices. Non-resident foreigners may qualify for the VAT exemption provided for in the Colombian Tax Code (Estatuto Tributario) when the requirements are met.
  • Care of the property. Guests are liable for any damage they cause to the room or common areas. We recommend not leaving valuables in plain sight.
  • Right of admission. We may refuse or terminate a stay for security reasons or for a serious breach of these rules, never on discriminatory grounds.
  • Platforms. If you book through Airbnb, Booking.com or another platform, their terms and cancellation policy also apply.
  • Complaints and claims. Write to us and we will respond within the legal time limits. You may also contact the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC) under Law 1480 of 2011.

Cancellation policy

Short (1–6 nights)Up to 72 hours before arrivalThe first night is charged

Long (30 nights or more)Up to 14 days before arrival50% of the deposit is retained
  • No-shows without prior notice are treated as late cancellations.
  • Early departure on short and medium stays: the nights used are charged at the rate corresponding to the actual length of stay. Long stays require 15 days’ written notice.
  • Force majeure (road closures, medical emergencies, natural disasters): we reschedule at no cost within the following 6 months.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days. International transfer fees are borne by the person requesting the transfer.

House rules

  • Check-in from 15:00 and check-out by 12:00.
  • Quiet hours from 22:00 to 07:00, out of respect for other guests and our neighbors.
  • No smoking or vaping inside the rooms or enclosed areas.
  • No parties, events or illegal substances.
  • Visitors must be announced and registered.
  • Pets only with prior authorization; service dogs are always welcome.
  • Help us save water and energy: switch off lights and appliances when you go out.
  • Help us recycle: sort your waste as indicated.

Prevention of the sexual exploitation of children and adolescents

Warning: pursuant to Article 17 of Law 679 of 2001, Camelot hereby informs its guests that the sexual exploitation and abuse of minors in Colombia are punishable under criminal and administrative law, in accordance with current legislation.

Camelot complies with Law 1336 of 2009 and the code of conduct against the commercial sexual exploitation of children and adolescents (ESCNNA). Minors are not admitted without their parents or an authorized adult. Report any suspicion: ICBF Line 141, National Police Line 123, or at teprotejo.org.

Accessibility

This site aims to meet the WCAG 2.2 Level AA guidelines: adequate contrast, keyboard navigation, alternative text and respect for reduced-motion preferences.

If you have mobility or other needs, let us know before booking: we’ll give you accurate information about access, stairs and the most suitable room.
