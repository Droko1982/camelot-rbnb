Frequently asked questions

What guests ask us most

Where is Camelot located?

In downtown Armenia, Quindío, a few blocks from Plaza de Bolívar, Calle Real and the banking district. We’ll send you the exact location and directions once your booking is confirmed.

What’s the difference between a short, medium and long stay?

It’s the same room at a different rate: short stays (1 to 6 nights) are charged per night; medium stays (7 to 29 nights) get 15% off; long stays (30 nights or more) have a monthly rate with utilities included and a written agreement.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 12:00. If you’re arriving earlier or leaving later, just message us and we’ll accommodate you based on availability.

Do the rooms have a private bathroom?

Yes. Every room is private, with its own bathroom, bed linen, towels and Wi-Fi.

Is the Wi-Fi good enough for work or study?

Yes, Wi-Fi is free for all guests. If your work depends on video calls, ask us for the room with the best signal.

How do I get there from the airport or the bus station?

From El Edén International Airport (AXM) it’s about 25 minutes by taxi; from Armenia’s Terminal de Transportes (bus station), about 10 minutes. We’ll send you directions when you book.

What payment methods do you accept?

We confirm the available methods when you book (bank transfer, digital payments and cash). Prices are settled in Colombian pesos; we show approximate amounts in US dollars and euros for reference.

Do you issue invoices?

Yes. Just request one when you book, with the details of the person or company. If you’re a non-resident foreigner, ask us about the VAT exemption on lodging provided for under Colombian tax law.

Are pets allowed?

Please check with us before booking. Service dogs are always welcome.

Do you have parking?

Ask us about availability. There are public parking lots a few blocks away downtown.

What is the cancellation policy?

Short stays: free cancellation up to 72 hours before arrival. Medium stays: up to 7 days before. Long stays: up to 14 days before. You’ll find the full details in our policies.

Is downtown Armenia safe?

It’s the busiest part of the city, with shops, banks and transportation. As in any city center, we recommend basic precautions at night; we’re happy to share tips when you arrive.
