Long stays in Armenia: your room by the month

A furnished private room in downtown Armenia, with utilities, Wi-Fi and housekeeping included. No guarantor, no co-signer and a clear written agreement.

Request my monthly proposal

Long stays

Who is it for?

Work assignments

Professionals, technicians and contractors coming to Armenia for a project or a construction job.

Health

Patients and their companions during treatment or recovery at the city’s clinics.

Study

Students, interns and researchers at Quindío’s universities, or anyone taking Spanish courses.

Remote work

Digital nomads looking for a mild climate, a good connection and a reasonable cost of living.

Relocation & paperwork

People moving to Armenia who need a base while they find a place to live.

Slow travelers

Retirees and slow travelers who want to experience the Coffee Region like a local.

Included in the monthly rate

  • Utilities (water, electricity and gas)
  • Wi-Fi

  • Weekly change of bed linen and towels

Requirements

  • Valid ID or passport

  • Refundable deposit agreed in writing

Frequently asked questions

Long-stay questions

Do I need a guarantor or co-signer?

No. We sign a lodging agreement with your details and a refundable deposit; we don’t ask for a guarantor, a co-signer or a rental background check.

Can I extend my stay?

Yes, subject to availability. Let us know at least 15 days in advance so we can hold your room.

What if I need to leave early?

Just give us 15 days’ written notice. The early departure terms are clearly set out in the agreement from the start.

Can I get an invoice in my company’s name?

Yes. We issue electronic invoices in the name of the person or company you specify.

When do I get my deposit back?

Within 5 business days after you check out, minus only damages or outstanding charges, if any.
